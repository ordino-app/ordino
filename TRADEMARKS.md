# The ordino name and logo

The code is MIT-licensed: you may use, change and redistribute it, also commercially.
The name **ordino** and the ordino logo are not covered by that licence. They identify the
official app from this repository, its update feed and its website.

- Forks and redistributed builds must use a different name and icon, and must not imply
  they are the official ordino.
- You may say your project is "based on ordino" or "a fork of ordino".
- Writing about ordino, linking to it and showing its unmodified logo next to it is fine.
